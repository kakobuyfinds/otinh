Shortlist and warehouse QC

  • Save the listing URL, seller SKU or variant name, color, size, quoted price and date. Keep that information together if you compare multiple listings.
  • For shoes, compare measurements using the same method as the listing. For clothing, compare flat chest width and body length with a garment that fits you.
  • When warehouse photos are available, compare the color, size label and visible condition with your order. Check the return window before approving shipment.
  • For shipping, compare quotes for the same destination, parcel dimensions and packed weight. Check which taxes or handling fees each quote includes.

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shortlist for tracking product links and comparing details such as seller options, sizing, quality check photos and likely delivery costs. This independent guide uses CAFANS listings as its product source.

Can I order these items through LitBuy?

Check the options on the CAFANS product page. If you plan to use LitBuy, confirm that it accepts the original seller link and supports your chosen variant and destination before paying.

What should I do if a product link has changed?

Open the matching CAFANS category and search for the product name. Confirm the seller, variant and current price before replacing the saved link in your shortlist.
